Nor on the other hand is it certain , however
much Alphonsus may affirm it to be so , "That
if Joseph was in England , it was after he had
been liberated from prison by Titus , and so
after the 80th year from the Lord's Nativity .
For it is certain that Jerusalem was destroyed by
Titus in the 70th year (not the 80th ) of the
Christian era : in fact , that which is assumed as
certain concerning Joseph of Arimathea being
liberated by him is an idle tale of the most frivo-
lous character .
