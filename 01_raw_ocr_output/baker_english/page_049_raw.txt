What was the outcome of this enquiry , I do
not find : but by those who have seen the Monas-
tery of Glaston it has been handed down , that
a chapel was there dedicated to the name of
Joseph , and also that a tomb was situated there
inscribed with this epitaph :
"I came to the Britons , after that I had
buried Christ :
I taught , I found rest ."
To which add also , if you please , the narrative
of William Goode the Jesuit , who in the reign
of Henry VIII was born at Glaston and was
educated there as a boy :
"There were in existence (he says ) at Glas-
conia inscribed tablets of brass to perpetuate his
memory , chapels , crypts , crosses, arms , and the
observance of the feast of S. Joseph for vi days

enjoyed most securely the King's Charters : now
