Caesar Baronius quotes from the Acts of
Mary Magdalene and her Companions on the
dispersion after the death of Stephan : "That
Lazarus , Mary Magdalene , Martha and Marcella ,
their attendant , against whom the Jews dis-
played the greater hatred , were not only driven
from Jerusalem but , in company with Maxi-
minus the disciple , were put into a boat without
oars and were consigned to the sure peril of the
sea: who by divine providence , they say , reached
Marseilles ."
