which are as follows : A white shield on which

arms or branches of the cross similar to the

and under the arms of the Cross is placed a
gilded chalice . And these were always denomi-
nated the insignia of St. Joseph who was piously
believed to have lived, and probably to have
been buried there ."
That the aforesaid Joseph was buried there
with his companions , I find thus recorded in the
"Great Register of the Monks of Glaston :"
"Here repose xii disciples : of whom the head
and guardian was Joseph of Arimathea , who
buried the Lord. Many from among the pagans
converted by their means to the faith of Christ
and baptized rest in the same spot , of whom on
account of their multitude , there is no number ."
