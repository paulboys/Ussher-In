GLASTONBURY TRADITIONS
concerning
JOSEPH OF ARIMATHEA
(Translated from Ussher's ‘ Antiquities ' Ch. ii)
I NOW Come to the traditions of the Monks of
Glaston concerning Joseph of Arimathea and
his companions : which , although I do not esteem
them more ancient than the coming of the Nor-
mans and are plainly redolent of the superstition
of later times , yet , of whatever quality they are , it
seemed well to me to set them out from the book
of William of Malmesbury on the " Antiquity
of the Church of Glaston ."
Thus , then, with a preliminary preface to
Henry of Blois- nephew to King Henry I, by
his sister Adela-then Bishop of Winchester and
Abbot of Glaston, he commences his archæology
from Apostolic times .
