Thus Polydore . And before him Richardus
Lepidus Bartholinus :
"Nor do I forget," says he , "that Joseph of
Arimathea , when he was shamefully imprisoned
by the Jews and so miraculously liberated by
Almighty God, was nowhere seen again in
Judea . For , as the writers of the annals shewed ,
he migrated into Britain ; and there he both taught
the worship of Christ and left many memorials
of himself in the Vale of Avalon , which can
there be seen .”
Thomas Scrope , at one time Bishop of Dro-
more in Ireland, relates the same thing concern-
ing the coming of Joseph , in the 7th Chapter of
the book which he wrote concerning the anti-
quity of the Order of the Carmelites .
And Thomas Harfeldius , in the "Summary of
the Kings of England ," where he says that
"Faganus and Duvianus (or Deruvianus ) in the
times of King Lucius found in the Isle of Avalon
an ancient church built by the disciples of Christ,
with ancient writings to the effect that after the
holy Apostles had dispersed in order to preach ,
St. Philip coming into France sent twelve of his
disciples into Britain of whom Joseph of Arima-
thea was the chief."
