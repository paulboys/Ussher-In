"For Joseph hath with him in his sarcophagus
two small silver and white vessels , filled with
Blood and Sweat of the Prophet Jesus . When
his sarcophagus shall be found entire , intact , in
time to come , it shall be seen ; and shall be open
unto all the world . Thenceforth nor water nor
the dew of heaven shall fail the dwellers in
that ancient isle .
"For a long while before the Day of Judgment
in Josaphat open shall these things be and de-
clared to living men ."
John Harding cites this Mevinus , the British
Chronicler , in his versified Chronicle , for whom
the name of Nenius appeared in the 48th Chapter :
where from him he relates the beautiful story,
how that Joseph delivered the Cross to King
Arviragus for the insignia of the British race
which , afterwards , was called the Georgian
Cross , depicted on a silver shield .
But I should abuse my leisure and the reader's
patience were I to recount the very frivolous
fictions which are referred to concerning Joseph
in the scattered writings of this kind , now by
Harding and Capgrave , now in the "Great
Register of Glaston ."
