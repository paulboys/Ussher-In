Of the twelve disciples sent by the Blessed
Philip into Britain , a certain monk of Malmes-
bury, in the second book of his "Eulogy ," makes
mention in this manner :
"In the LXIII year from the Lord's Incarna-
tion and the XV from the Assumption of the
Blessed Virgin Mary twelve disciples came into
Greater Britain sent thither by the Blessed
Apostle Philip to preach the word of God : of
whom the chief was Joseph of Arimathea who
buried Jesus Christ . On whom the King Arvira-
gus , although a pagan , in order that they might
toil no longer, conferred XII hides of land in the
Isle of Avalon which is now called Glastonia .
