"That Joseph who , on the testimony of the
Evangelist Matthew, born in the city of Arima-
thea, had buried the body of Christ, whether by
chance or design , came by the Will of God ,
with no small retinue into Britain where , as well
he as his Companions when they preached
concerning the Gospel and assiduously taught
the doctrine of Christ, many were led by their
means to the true faith and strengthened with
its health -giving fruit , were baptized . These men ,
assuredly inspired by the Holy Spirit , when they
accepted as a gift from the King a little piece of
land for their habitation (nigh to the town of
Wells , about 4,000 paces ) , there they laid the
first foundations of the new religion , where
to -day is a magnificent sanctuary and a noble
community of the monastic family of the Order
of S. Benedict .
"The name of the place is Glasconia . This con-
stituted absolutely the first beginnings of the
Christian faith in Britain which , later on, though
nearly extinguished , King Lucius rekindled-
being sustained by the fountain of baptism — and
piously augmented , as we shall hereafter relate.
For Gildas is witness that the British received
the Christian religion from the very beginning
of the rise of the Gospel."
