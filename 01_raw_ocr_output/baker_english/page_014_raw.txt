the priests of the Jews , with the Pharisees and
Scribes , inflamed with the fires of envy , stirred
up a persecution in the Church, by slaying the
protomartyr , Stephan , and driving afar nearly all
from their borders .
"Whilst , therefore , this storm of persecution
was raging , the scattered believers sought divers
kingdoms of the earth , chosen by the Lord for
Himself , in order to deliver the Word of Salva-
tion to the nations . Now St. Philip , as Frecul-
phus testifieth in his second book , Chapter IV,
coming into the region of the Franks in order to
preach , converted many to the faith and baptized
them . Thereupon, purposing to spread Christ's
Word, he chose twelve from among his disciples ,
and sent them into Britain to preach the Gospel
of the Word of Life : over whom he placed in
authority , it is said , his dearest friend, Joseph of
Arimathea , who buried the Lord .
