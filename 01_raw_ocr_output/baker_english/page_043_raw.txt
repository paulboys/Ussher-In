As to the third point , that the whole of the
realm of Britain did not receive the faith by the
ministry of Joseph , it is true enough but it is not
relevant to the issue . For neither do the Spanish
themselves say that the whole kingdom of Spain
was converted by James ; nor is it established
that whole kingdoms were made Christian by
any one of the Apostles : and , moreover , the
question at issue concerns the antiquity , not the
universality , of the reception of the faith .
As to the fourth and last point , this rests
partly upon doubtful , and partly upon false ,
premises . Rodericus Ximensis , Archbishop of
Toledo , accounted the preaching of James in
Spain as doubtful , in the controversy which he
had in the year 1215 , in the Lateran Council
with the Prefect of Compostella , concerning the
