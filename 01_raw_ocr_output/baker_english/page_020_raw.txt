Now, as for Philip having used the aid of
Joseph of Arimathea in bringing the people of
Britain to the faith , this is to be drawn from the
wells of knowledge (lacunis) of the Monks of
Glaston from which indeed have originated these
rude verses quoted out of certain chronicles
concerning King Arviragus :
"There enters Avallonia a band of twelve men ;
Of these the flower and chief is Joseph of
Arimathea .

Father :
