IV. "It is still affirmed , moreover , granted
that which is denied , the Spanish first received
the faith , which is proved, because James suffered
martyrdom in the life of Peter . For it is written
of Herod , that he slew James with the sword, and
had the intention to apprehend Peter , Chapter
XII of the Acts . But it is well known that Peter
suffered under Nero . Nero , moreover , was about
the year of Our Lord 54 and Titus about the
year 80. Now it is certain that if Joseph were in
England, this was after he was liberated from
prison by Titus , and thus after the 80th year from
the Nativity of Our Lord : and James died many
years before Nero , about the 40th year from the
Lord's nativity.
"And Spain received the faith from James , in
his lifetime , or immediately on his burial ; as
above stated. Therefore Spain is the more
ancient in the faith."
Thus Alphonsus Garcias to the Fathers at the
Council of Basle : wherein some things are wholly
trivial , not a few exceedingly ridiculous.
