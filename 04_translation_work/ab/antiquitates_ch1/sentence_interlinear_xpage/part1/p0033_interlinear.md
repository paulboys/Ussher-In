## Reading

They were crushed like fish and locusts, like flies and gnats; for without knowledge of his Creator, every man is but a beast. Now, however, the passion of Christ and his resurrection resound in the voices and the writings of all peoples.”

For when the fullness of time had come, “the mystery that had been kept silent through the ages was made manifest, and by the command of the eternal God was declared through the prophetic Scriptures to all peoples, to bring them to the obedience of faith.” From this, that light, destined to be revealed to the nations, dawned upon those who sat in darkness and the shadow of death. Of this God speaks through the prophet: “I have appointed you to be a light to the peoples, that you may bring salvation to the ends of the earth.” For when Christ commanded his apostles to go out into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature, they went out and preached everywhere, leaving no place hidden from the heavenly teaching; they reached, as Gregory of Nyssa says, “μὴ νήσους, μὴ ἤπειρον, μηδ' εἴτινα τρίτην ἀνθρώποις κατοικίαν ἡ φύσις ἐδίδου—neither the islands, nor the mainland, nor any third dwelling-place that nature had granted to humankind.”

And so was fulfilled what God had once foretold through Isaiah: “I will send some of them to the peoples across the ocean, and to the most remote islands, which had not heard my fame nor seen my glory, that they may declare my glory among the nations.” For “the word of God,” in Augustine’s own words, “has been preached not on the continental land alone, but also in the islands set in the midst of the sea; and these too are full of Christians, full of the servants of God. For the sea does not separate them from him who made the sea.”
