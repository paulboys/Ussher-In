**EN**  ...of the eternal God, declared to all the nations for the obedience of faith.” And from this, upon those who had been placed in darkness and the shadow of death, that light shone forth which was to be revealed to the nations — concerning which God speaks in the prophet: “I have appointed you to be a light of the nations, that you may be for salvation unto the ends of the earth.” For when Christ commanded his apostles that, going forth into the whole world, they should preach the gospel to every creature, they went out and preached everywhere, so that they allowed no place to remain hidden from the heavenly teaching: “μὴ νήσους, μὴ ἤπειρον, μηδʼ εἴτινα τρίτην ἀνθρώποις κατοικίαν ἡ φύσις ἐδίδου [neither the islands, nor the mainland, nor any third dwelling-place that nature had granted to humankind],” as Gregory of Nyssa says. And thus was fulfilled what God had long before foretold through Isaiah: “I will send from among them to the nations in the ocean, and to the most remote islands, which had not heard my fame nor seen my glory, that they may declare my glory among the nations.” For “the word of God” — these are Augustine’s words — “has been preached not on the mainland alone, but also in the islands which are set in the midst of the sea; and these too are full of Christians, full of the servants of God. For the sea does not separate them from him who had made the sea.
