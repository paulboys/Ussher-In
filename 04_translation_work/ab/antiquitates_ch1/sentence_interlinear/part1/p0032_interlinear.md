ANTIQUITIES OF THE BRITISH CHURCHES.

CHAPTER I. The Britons were idolaters before they received the Christian faith. On the apostles who are said to have first preached the gospel in the British Isles: James the son of Zebedee, Simon the Zealot, Simon Peter, and the blessed Paul. Likewise on Aristobulus, and Claudia the Briton.

No one need doubt that in those times of ancient ignorance which came before the saving advent of Christ, our Britons and Irish shared the common lot of the rest of the peoples — that they were, in short, "without Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope, and without God in the world." For as the impious Porphyry once remarked, "neither had Britain, that province so fertile in tyrants, nor the Scottish peoples, nor any of the barbarous nations round about as far as the ocean, come to know Moses and the prophets."
