## Interlinear

**LA**  Punici scriptore: quo consulatum gerente, Neronem in teriisse, et alii authores confirmant, et ipse Martialis signi ficat illis de Silio versiculis : Postquam bis senis ingentem fascibus annum Rexerat, asserto qui sacer orbe fuit : Emeritos Musis et Phœbo tradidit annos, Proque suo celebrat nunc Helicona foro.

**EN**  the author of the Punica; and that Nero perished during his consulship is confirmed both by other authorities and by Martial himself, who indicates as much in those verses about Silius: After he had governed the mighty year with its twice-six fasces — he who was hallowed when the world had been set free — he devoted his remaining years to the Muses and to Phoebus, and now frequents Helicon in place of his own forum.

**LA**  Nerone vero summam rerum obtinente (et quidem, ut pluribus placet, sub finem imperii illius) a Paulo illa scripta est epistola, in qua Timotheum salutant Pudens et Linus et Claudia. Sed de his in capite tertio iterum etiam erit dicendi locus.

**EN**  But while Nero held supreme power — and indeed, as most hold, toward the end of his reign — that epistle was written by Paul in which Pudens, Linus, and Claudia send their greetings to Timothy. But there will be occasion to speak of these again in the third chapter.

## Reading

Silius Italicus, the author of the Punica; and that Nero perished during his consulship is confirmed both by other authorities and by Martial himself, who indicates as much in those verses about Silius:<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0045_fn_001">r</a></sup> After he had governed the mighty year with its twice-six fasces — he who was hallowed when the world had been set free — he devoted his remaining years to the Muses and to Phoebus, and now frequents Helicon in place of his own forum.

While Nero held supreme power — and indeed, as most hold, toward the end of his reign — Paul wrote that epistle in which Pudens, Linus, and Claudia send their greetings to Timothy. But there will be occasion to speak of these again in the third chapter.
