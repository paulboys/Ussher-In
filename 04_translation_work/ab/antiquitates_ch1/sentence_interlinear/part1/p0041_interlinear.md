**EN**  this, however, I report on the testimony of others, for I myself have not yet had the opportunity to see the author. The old translator of Chrysostom's sermon on Pentecost likewise intimates that he ran from Spain "into the farthest parts" of the earth. Finally, "like some heavenly bird ranging round about the inhabited world, having completed thirty-five years of gospel warfare for Christ," as Nicephorus writes, he at last consummated the course of his martyrdom at Rome under Nero.
