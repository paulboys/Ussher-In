What he says about the latest period of Tiberius must be explained from Eusebius's Chronicle, in which, under the last or next-to-last year of Tiberius, it is recorded: "When Pilate reported to Tiberius concerning the doctrine of the Christians, Tiberius referred the matter to the senate, that it might be received among the other sacred rites. But when, by decree of the senators, it had been resolved that the Christians be expelled from the city, Tiberius by edict threatened death to their accusers. Tertullian writes this in his Apology."

The words of Tertullian<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0035_fn_001">t</a></sup> are these: "Tiberius, in whose time the Christian name entered the world, when news had been reported to him from Syria Palaestina that there the truth of that divinity had been revealed, referred it to the senate with the prerogative of his own vote. The senate, because it had not itself approved it, rejected it. Caesar held to his opinion, threatening danger to the accusers of the Christians."

Eusebius adds, in the second book of the Ecclesiastical History, chapter three, in Rufinus's translation: "This indeed divine providence then so instilled into Caesar's mind that, without any obstacle, in the very first beginnings the word of the Gospel ran everywhere. From this it also came about that, as if a light suddenly displayed from heaven, or some ray of the sun bursting forth, it lit up the whole world with the brightness of the light from on high — so that the prophecy was fulfilled which had said, 'Their sound went out into<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0035_fn_002">u</a></sup> all the earth' — namely the evangelists' and the apostles' — 'and their words to the ends of the world.' From this also, throughout all cities and villages, immense multitudes were gathered to the churches, just as at harvest time the grain is gathered to the threshing-floors."

"The gathering<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0035_fn_003">w</a></sup> of the Christians penetrated into every kind of men; and no nation of the Roman world was left without its worship."

<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0035_fn_004">x</a></sup>Μία ἡ ἐκκλησία, ἣν ἱδρύσαντο οἱ ἅγιοι ἀπόστολοι ἀπὸ περάτων ἕως περάτων ἐν τῷ αἵματι τοῦ Χριστοῦ, οἰκείοις ἱδρῶσι καὶ πόνοις: there is one Church, which the holy apostles founded from end to end in the blood of Christ, by their own sweat and toil.
