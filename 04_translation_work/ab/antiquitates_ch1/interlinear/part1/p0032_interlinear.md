## Reading

OF THE BRITISH CHURCHES

ANTIQUITIES.

CHAPTER I.

The Britons, before they received the Christian faith, were idolaters. On the apostles said to have first preached the gospel in the British isles: James the son of Zebedee, Simon the Zealot, Simon Peter, and the blessed Paul. Likewise on Aristobulus and Claudia the Briton.

No one need doubt that in the times of that ancient ignorance<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0032_fn_001">a</a></sup> which preceded the saving advent of Christ, our Britons and Irish shared the common lot of the rest of the peoples — that is, they were "without Christ,<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0032_fn_006">b</a></sup> aliens from the commonwealth of Israel, strangers as regards the covenants of promise, having no hope, and without God in the world."
