## Reading

...presbyters and deacons, returned again to Rome in the twelfth year of Nero Caesar."

Of St. Paul, the teacher of the Gentiles—τὸν<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_001">t</a></sup> τὴν οἰκουμένην σταδεύσαντα, καὶ τῷ περὶ πίστεως δρόμῳ τὸν κόσμον μικρὸν ἀποφήναντα, as Basil of Seleucia speaks of him—Jerome<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_002">u</a></sup> affirms that he "ran from ocean to ocean," "imitating his Lord and the Sun of righteousness, of whom we read<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_003">w</a></sup>: 'His going forth is from the end of heaven, and his circuit reaches to its farthest end'—so that the earth would sooner fail him than his zeal for preaching." And after the first defense, which Paul himself mentions in his second epistle to Timothy, he was released by Nero and went on to preach the gospel<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_004">x</a></sup> of Christ in the western regions as well.

The apostle's<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_005">y</a></sup> own words are: "In my first defense no one stood with me; but all forsook me. May it not be charged against them. But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the preaching might be fulfilled, and all the Gentiles might hear." On this passage Theodoret comments: "When, having made his appeal, he was sent to Rome by Festus, and his defense having been heard, he was acquitted, and set out for Spain, and, hastening on to other peoples, brought to them the light of doctrine"; and, as the same author adds elsewhere<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_006">z</a></sup>, "To the islands that lie in the sea he brought benefit."

Whence Venantius Fortunatus<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_007">a</a></sup> writes of him:

He crossed the Ocean, even where the island forms a harbor,
and the lands the Briton holds, and those of farthest Thule.

And Sophronius, patriarch of Jerusalem, in his sermon on the birthday of the apostles, indicates that Paul preached the gospel to the Spaniards and the Britons—which, however, on the testimony of others<sup><a href="#fn-seg_p0040_fn_008">b</a></sup>
